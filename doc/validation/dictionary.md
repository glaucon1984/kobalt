# Dictionary checks

Label `dictionary`. Build `mac-tests`, then run `ctest --preset mac -L dictionary` from `platforms/desktop/`. `ctest --preset mac -N -L dictionary` lists its tests.

These checks cover word lookup, dictionary editing, dictionary book search, downloaded dictionaries, the dictionary chooser and dictionary rendering. `model.dictionary-store` uses controlled network replies and needs no live download. `component.ui`, in [Settings and controls](settings.md), checks the dictionary rows, the chooser, the editor, definition rendering and the keyboard refresh policy.

`model.saved-words` checks My Words storage: case variants of a word share one row, a repeated lookup counts and keeps its first sentence, removal, one book's list against every book's list, relocation after a move, words saved without a dictionary entry, which never replace a saved definition, Unicode and length limits, the migration from schema version 3 and rejection of a future version. `desktop.savedWords` uses a test dictionary that defines every word. It looks a word up through the reader and checks the sheet's save cell between search and the dictionary picker. The save cell uses the shared checkbox header button, with an open-book icon and a lower-right checkbox. The checks verify that its checked state follows saving and removal. It opens the Words tab, the word view with its underlined sentence and page button, and every book's words. It cancels and then confirms the removal of the word, then saves it again with the sheet's cell. It saves a name the test dictionary has no entry for and checks that the Words tab says the reader added it. It also checks that saving after switching dictionaries keeps the displayed definition, the exact add and remove feedback, automatic dismissal, and that disabling My Words hides saving and prevents automatic recording. These checks run in light and dark themes. `desktop.dictionaryBookSearch` checks that only the portrait phone sheet replaces edit, search and save with one menu cell, and searches and edits through that menu there. The `myWords` gallery case seeds words with fixed dates, including one from a book that is not on the device. It captures the saved and unsaved lookup, the add and remove feedback, the Words tab, the word view, every book's words and a word whose book is missing.

The `desktop.plugin*` checks point the reader at fixture Lua plugins in `tests/fixtures/plugins/reader/`. They check that the definition opens first while the plugins are asked, that a card adds a marker to the dictionary chooser and an entry under its Plugins heading, that choosing the entry shows the card and choosing the dictionary shows the definition again, that choosing the definition that is already shown keeps it, that the choice is not remembered for the next word, that a card arriving after the sheet opened still adds its entry, and that editing the word asks the plugins again. They also cover a plugin with no card, a failing plugin, a card that arrives after a tap dismissed the sheet, a page turn, a lookup cancelled by suspension and a host without a plugin folder. The late-card and suspension checks act at the moment the reader posts the request, read from the reader's `reader_plugin_requests` property, so the reply always arrives afterwards. `model.lua-runtime`, `model.plugin-runtime`, `model.plugin-host`, `model.plugin-store` and `model.plugin-position` check the Lua sandbox with its limits on C library calls, JSON, files, modules, cards and logging; the stores with their limits, including values whose shared tables would expand past them, file format and backups, and a bad file that cannot be moved aside; the plugin thread with its folder rule, one card per plugin as it is ready, skipped stale lookups, statuses, switches, saving at close and sleep, deleting data and bounded wait at close; and the reading position that plugins receive, capped after a link jump.

The `dictionary-plugin-choice` and `dictionary-plugin-card` gallery scenes capture the chooser with its Plugins heading and the card it opens, next to the `dictionary-definition` scene in the same sheet. Capture them with `KOBALT_GALLERY_CASE=dictionary` until a full gallery has indexed them.

These checks use a controlled keyboard. They do not establish physical Kobo or Android keyboard and touch behavior.

`model.dictionary-store` also checks selected-folder StarDict preparation, lookup, stable IDs, missing source files and coexistence with downloaded dictionaries. Index generation uses only the private working copy. Invalid dictionaries are omitted from the folder catalogue.
